# Airmonlink Business Manager — User Guide

**Product:** Airmonlink Business Manager  
**Current release:** 1.3.0 Build 9  
**Platform:** Windows 10 / Windows 11 (64-bit)  
**Purpose:** Offline-first point of sale and business management

This guide is written for business owners, managers, cashiers, accountants, stock officers, and other authorised staff who use Airmonlink Business Manager.

> Important: Your business data is valuable. Create regular backups and keep at least one backup on a different device or secure remote location.
